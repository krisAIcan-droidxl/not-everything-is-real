# Art direction

## Thesis
Not Everything Is Real is an interactive campaign about inherited programming, media influence, polarization, awareness, empathy and choosing one's own reality.

## Visual language
Predominantly black/charcoal/white. Restrained red is warning/emotional pressure; cyan is signal/information. Photographic humans, wet reflective surfaces, volumetric light, deep blacks, subtle grain. No Matrix code, hacker terminals, futuristic city, generic cyberpunk or decorative particle spam.

## Narrative spine
PROGRAMMED → POLARIZED → AWARE → UNLEARN → REPROGRAM → HUMAN.

## Campaign language
DON'T BELIEVE THE NOISE. BELIEVE IN YOURSELF.
BECOME YOURS.
DIFFERENT DOESN'T MEAN DIVIDED.
KINDNESS IS A SKILL.
BEFORE THE LABEL, THERE WAS A PERSON.
CREATE YOUR REALITY.
