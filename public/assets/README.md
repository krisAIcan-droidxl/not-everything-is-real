Gate 02 hybrid materials

person-atlas.webp: generated photographic material sheet following the approved full-body young-man reference: identical front, back, left and right views, curly hair, oversized charcoal shirt, loose black cargo trousers and grey sneakers, relaxed standing pose, transparent background. No typography, glitch or fragmentation baked into the asset. Projected onto the closed garment surface in human-material.js; the camera and construction remain spatial WebGL geometry.

wet-concrete.webp: generated seamless overhead photograph of almost-black wet concrete, subtle puddle boundaries, grit and small debris, flat neutral illumination. Used for ground albedo, roughness and bump.

Both assets were generated with the built-in image tool and encoded as WebP for delivery. Atlas coordinates refer to the original 1672 × 941 sheet. The reflected human shares the constructed surface and scroll uniforms; there is no reflection render pass or post-processing.
